# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the DANOS-Open project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, or level of experience.

## Our Standards
Constructive criticism, technical rigor, and kindness are expected.
Trolling, insulting, and personal or political attacks are not
tolerated.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the project team
(see SECURITY.md for contact). All complaints are reviewed and
addressed; the project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality.
